A structured archive of **Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE)** coursework, lecture slides, lab manuals, reference textbooks, white papers, and capstone reports spanning **Second Year through Fourth Year** (Semesters III–VIII), alongside a curated **Technical Resources** library.

## [Technical Resources](./TECHNICAL%20RESOURCES) (`19` files)

A supplementary collection of practical handbooks and reference texts for hardware, embedded systems, and robotics:

- **[Electronics](./TECHNICAL%20RESOURCES/ELECTRONICS)** (`8` PDFs) — *Basic Electronics* (Debashis De), *Electronics All-in-One For Dummies*, *Practical Electronics*, transistor radio & low-power transmitter project guides.

- **[IoT and Robotics](./TECHNICAL%20RESOURCES/IOT%20AND%20ROBOTICS)** (`2` PDFs) — *5G-Enabled Internet of Things* and *Artificial Intelligence for Robotics*.
- **[Microcontrollers](./TECHNICAL%20RESOURCES/MICROCONTROLLERS)** (`3` PDFs) — *Advanced Programming with STM32*, *NodeMCU Development Workshop*, and *PIC Microcontrollers: 50 Projects for Beginners and Experts*.
- **[Power Electronics](./TECHNICAL%20RESOURCES/POWER%20ELECTRONICS)** (`1` PDF) — *Power Electronics Basics*.
